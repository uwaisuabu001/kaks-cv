The Profile

pp. 02–03

A one-sentence headline about you: what you do, and the kind of work you want to be known for.

Write your story here in two or three short paragraphs. Start with where you are now: what you study or do day to day, and what drew you to it in the first place.

Then say what you are good at, the kind of problems you enjoy, and the sort of team you work well in. Keep sentences short; editors cut everything else.

End with what you are looking for next: a role, an internship, freelance work, a collaboration.
